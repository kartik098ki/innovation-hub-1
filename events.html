About the Flagship Event

Connecting Student Innovators with Institutional Capital

Ridehack 2026 is the signature startup accelerator and pitching showcase organized by the Innovation Hub at Jaypee Institute of Information Technology (JIIT Sector 128 Noida). We identify, fund, and support visionary student founders building market-ready solutions across deep-tech, artificial intelligence, enterprise software, hardware, and climate resilience.

🏛️

JIIT 128 Incubation

Physical workspace, prototyping labs, computing resources, and faculty guidance.

💰

Non-Dilutive Grants

Category-based cash awards across 5 tracks to build and deploy your working MVP.

🚀

VC & Angel Syndicates

Fast-tracked intros to leading venture capital funds, institutional partners, and angels.

🤝

1-on-1 Pitch Clinics

Intensive deck refinement and unit economics coaching for shortlisted finalist cohorts.

Pitch Domains

THE 5 SECTOR TRACKS

Review each track's problem spaces, eligibility, and evaluation parameters. Teams may submit their venture in the track that best aligns with their primary core technology.

Track 01

AI & Autonomous DeepTech

Foundation Models, Agentic Workflows, Computer Vision & Generative Intelligence

Apply to Track ↗

Scope & Problem Spaces

Multi-agent collaboration, domain-specific LLM fine-tuning, automated code analysis, speech synthesis, autonomous edge vision, and neuro-symbolic reasoning.

Evaluation Criteria

Algorithm novelty, inference latency, data moat, business viability, and functional prototype demonstrations.

Track Incentives

Non-dilutive seed grant pool, GPU compute credits, direct intros to AI-focused venture syndicates.

Track 02

FinTech, DeFi & Open Finance

Cross-Border Rails, Algorithmic Underwriting, Micro-Lending & Web3 Infrastructure

Apply to Track ↗

Scope & Problem Spaces

Real-time fraud detection, decentralized identity, programmable cross-border payments, compliance tooling, and alternative credit underwriting models.

Evaluation Criteria

Security posture, regulatory compliance awareness, unit margins, and distribution strategy.

Track Incentives

Banking API sandbox credits, regulatory mentorship, and term sheet evaluations from FinTech syndicates.

Track 03

Enterprise SaaS & Cloud Infra

Developer Tooling, Data Engineering, Cybersecurity & Workflow Automation

Apply to Track ↗

Scope & Problem Spaces

Distributed cloud architecture, observability platforms, CI/CD automated security testing, enterprise knowledge graphs, and privacy-preserving data sharing.

Evaluation Criteria

Customer validation, ARR pipeline potential, enterprise integration friction, and system scalability.

Track Incentives

Major cloud hosting credits, pilot introductions with enterprise tech leaders, and cash grant pool.

Track 04

Robotics, Hardware & Smart Drones

Autonomous Aerial Systems, Embedded Edge Systems, Industrial Automation & IoT

Apply to Track ↗

Scope & Problem Spaces

Industrial warehouse robotics, agricultural UAV surveillance, sensory edge telemetry, smart grid automation, and wearable biomedical devices.

Evaluation Criteria

Hardware bill-of-materials (BOM) feasibility, prototype durability, field validation, and power efficiency.

Track Incentives

JIIT 128 hardware prototyping lab access, manufacturing mentors, and component procurement support.

Track 05

CleanTech, Climate & AgriTech

Renewable Energy Systems, Carbon Accounting, Precision AgriSensors & Circular Tech

Apply to Track ↗

Scope & Problem Spaces

Battery management intelligence, carbon tracking ERP, solar micro-inverter monitoring, farm yield optimization, and wastewater circular recovery.

Evaluation Criteria

Measurable carbon or resource impact, unit economics at scale, deployment feasibility in Indian markets.

Track Incentives

Green technology grants, incubation at JIIT Sector 128, and sustainability partner fund intros.

Eligibility & Team Guidelines

  • • Open to student innovators, collegiate teams, and early-stage startup founders across India.
  • • Team size: 1 to 5 members per startup registration.
  • • Startups must submit a pitch deck (PDF) outlining problem, solution, TAM, business model, and prototype video/link.
  • • Shortlisted top 30 finalists must attend the offline grand finale pitch on 01 Nov 2026 at JIIT Sector 128 Noida.

Key Deadlines & Dates

Cohort Shortlist Announced 22 OCT 2026
Grand Offline Pitching Finale 01 NOV 2026
