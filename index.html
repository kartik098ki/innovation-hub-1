The Manifesto

Where Innovation Meets
Market Reality

The Jaypee Institute of Information Technology (JIIT) Innovation community connects technical talent with the world of entrepreneurship and venture creation. Over the years, we have created platforms that encourage learning, meaningful dialogue, and real opportunities for growth.

Ridehack is at the heart of this vision. As JIIT's premier startup pitching event, Ridehack is a platform connecting early-stage startups and student founders directly with angel investors, VCs, grant providers, and industry mentors to connect, reflect, and shape ideas that truly matter and turn them into lasting impact.
